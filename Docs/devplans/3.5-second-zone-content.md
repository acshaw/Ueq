# 3.5 — Second Zone (Thornwood Content)

**Status:** ✅ Done — verified in-editor 2026-07-13. TW1–TW9 applied as recommended.

## Goal
Turn Thornwood from a 280u flat scaffold (one `SpawnPoint` mobId="Giant Rat", built by `ZoneSetup.BuildZoneScenes`
in 3.0.2) into the first **real second zone**: a shaped forest matching its design intent, populated with an
actual encounter design (goblin patrols), proving the 3.1.9–3.1.11 authoring tooling generalizes past Creslin's
Field. Grounded directly in `Assets/Scripts/trellis_zone_design.md`'s "Zone 2: Thornwood" section (the only
design doc for this zone — quoted throughout below). Full design-doc depth (levels 6–15, Shaman/Warchief,
wildlife, named encounters) is explicitly **not** the bar here — see TW1.

Also closes the roadmap 3.5 line's "defines what state crosses zone boundaries in practice" question — see TW9:
this was already answered by 3.0/3.0.2/3.1.11(WR7); 3.5 just needs to keep holding with real content in both
zones at once, not invent anything new.

## Current state (grounding)
- Thornwood = scene `Assets/Scenes/Zones/thornwood.unity`, zone id `thornwood`, world offset `+5000,0,0`,
  built by `ZoneSetup.BuildFlatZone` (a flat 280u `Plane` named `thornwood_Ground`, `NavMeshSurface` baked +
  persisted to `thornwood_navmesh.asset`). Markers: `ZoneEntry("default")` at offset+(0,0,-8),
  `ZoneEntry("from_grukmar")` at offset+(0,0,8), `ZonePortal→creslins_field` at offset+(0,0,-16),
  `ZonePortal→grukmars_deep` at offset+(0,0,16), one `SpawnPoint(mobId="Giant Rat")` at offset+(6,0,0).
  **Re-running `Tools/Zones/Build Zone Scenes` wipes all of this and rebuilds from an empty scene** — 3.5 work
  must happen by opening `thornwood.unity` directly and building on top of it, never by re-running that tool.
- The three shaping/dressing tools built for Creslin's Field (`CreslinsFieldSetup` → flat reshape + perimeter;
  `TerrainZoneSetup` → procedural heightmap terrain w/ west-cliff/east-mountain/north-treeline shaping,
  `TerrainTextureSetup`'s Synty-splat material, persisted NavMesh) are **hardcoded to the active/base scene**
  (`GameObject.Find("Ground")`, `FindFirstObjectByType<NetworkStartPosition>()`, `Assets/Scenes/SampleScene/
  Terrain/` asset path) and to **Creslin's-specific geography** (west=ocean, east=mountains, north=forest→
  Thornwood). None of them run against a non-base additive zone scene as-is.
- The **decoration + encounter tooling** built in 3.1.9–3.1.11 (`ScatterWindow`, `PoiBuilder`,
  `TerrainPathPainter`, `TrellisHubSetup`, `EncounterTools` [`Place Encounter`/`New Patrol Route`/`Add Patrol
  Waypoint`/`New Wander Region`], `EncounterGizmos`) place at the **Scene-view focus point** or a **selected
  object**, snap to navmesh/ground via raycast, and have **no Creslin's-specific assumptions** — these work
  in Thornwood today, unchanged, once it's the open scene.
- Mob/spawn content is fully DB-driven (2.3–2.7.2): web Mob Editor, Spawn Editor (weighted tables +
  `groupSize`), Faction Editor. `MobModelCatalog` maps a mob id → a Synty body prefab by convention. Owned
  bodies in `Assets/Synty/PolygonDungeon/Prefabs/Characters/`: `Goblin_Male_01`, `Goblin_Female_01`,
  `Goblin_Shaman_01`, `Goblin_WarChief_01`, `Goblin_Warrior_Male_01`, `Goblin_Warrior_Female_01` (+
  Skeleton/Ghost/Golem/Hero Knight variants used elsewhere). **No wolf/bear/predator body in any owned pack**
  — see TW8.
- Existing DB mob `Goblin Scout` (level 2, body `Goblin_Male_01`) was seeded for Creslin's Field example
  encounters (3.1.10) and is reusable here — a mob isn't zone-exclusive, any `SpawnPoint` in any zone can
  reference it.
- `PatrolBehavior`/`PatrolRoute` (3.1.10) + `WanderRegion` (3.1.11, box/sphere/free-range) are zone-agnostic
  `INpcMovementBehavior`s. `SpawnPoint.DoSpawn` loops `groupSize` times and calls `ApplyPatrol` per spawned
  mob — so a patrol "group" is N mobs independently walking the **same** route (jittered spawn offset), not a
  single cohesive formation. Good enough to read as an organized patrol; not literal group cohesion.

## Design doc quote (Zone 2: Thornwood)
> Dense forest zone. Southern boundary: treeline bordering Creslin's Field (zone transition). Eastern
> boundary: mountain range face — impassable, vertical cliff geography. Western boundary: coastal cliffs, no
> beach access. Northern boundary: deep forest, zone edge. Grukmar's Deep entrance: carved into the mountain
> face, eastern boundary, mid-north position. ... No settlements, no safe areas. ... Goblin patrols: mixed
> Goblin Male and Female scouts moving in groups of 2-4. Goblin Warrior Male and Female: closer to the dungeon
> entrance, organized patrol routes. ... Zone sweeper: a large predator... Difficulty gradient: southern
> treeline 6-9, mid forest 8-12, northern deep forest 10-14, dungeon entrance approach 12-15.

## Decisions

### TW1 — Scope bar: MVP proof-of-framework, not full design-doc depth — **RECOMMEND**
3.5's job (per roadmap) is to prove the zone framework + authoring tooling on a **second** real zone, not to
build Thornwood to its eventual full depth (that's years of content across multiple future milestones as
levels/combat/Grukmar exist). **In scope:** real terrain shape matching the four-boundary geography, the
signature encounter (goblin patrols in the southern/mid band), a `WanderRegion`-bounded scout presence,
NavMesh + portal graph intact, an authentic Grukmar-entrance placement. **Out of scope (→ 3.6 / backlog):**
mid/deep/entrance-approach difficulty bands (need Grukmar + higher-level content to matter), Goblin Shaman /
Warchief (explicitly Zone 3 population per the doc), the zone sweeper + any wildlife (TW8 — no owned art),
named patrol leader rare-spawn + drop content, Aldric's ranger/warrior group-hail variants (needs
class-conditional group-hail dialogue, not built), atmosphere/lighting (canopy darkness), quest content.

### TW2 — Terrain tool: a new sibling script, not a generalized system — **RECOMMEND**
Don't generalize `TerrainZoneSetup`/`CreslinsFieldSetup` into an N-zone parameterized system — there are only
two outdoor zones total (Grukmar's Deep is an underground dungeon needing modular-kit tooling, not a
heightmap), so a generic "zone terrain shaper" would be speculative abstraction for a system with one other
caller. Instead: **`ThornwoodTerrainSetup.cs`**, a sibling following the same recipe (procedural heightmap →
`TerrainTextureSetup.ApplyToTerrain` → persisted `NavMeshSurface` → border dressing), with its own
`ShapeHeight`/`BuildBorders` matching Thornwood's geography (TW3) and its own spawn-origin resolution (the
zone's `ZoneEntry("default")`, not `NetworkStartPosition` — Thornwood has no player spawn). **Do** factor the
genuinely-shared, already-proven-correct primitives that would otherwise be copy-pasted byte-for-byte (`Fbm`/
`SStep` noise, the "size heightmap resolution before reading it back" bake-and-persist recipe, footprint
measuring, collider stripping) into a small shared `TerrainShapingUtil.cs` used by both scripts — these aren't
new abstractions, they're extracting logic that took multiple painful iterations to get right (see the 2026-07-06
CLAUDE.md saga: the corner-fill heightmap bug, the alpha-smoothness gotcha) and must not be re-solved slightly
wrong for zone 2. `TerrainTextureSetup`'s Synty-splat material (the one that actually shipped, after the
custom-shader/texture experiments were reverted) is already shared-shaped — just call it again.

### TW3 — Thornwood's four-boundary shape — **RECOMMEND**
Adapt Creslin's Field's proven shaping approach, reusing what transfers directly and writing new logic only
where the geography differs:
- **East (mountain face, impassable)** — same steep/tall wall function as Creslin's east edge (reuse the
  `east²`-ramped exponent approach). **Notch it once**, mid-north, down to a walkable saddle for the Grukmar
  cave-mouth entrance (TW4) — the one genuinely new shaping wrinkle.
- **West (coastal cliffs, no beach access)** — same steep cliff→sea-level descent + water plane as Creslin's
  west edge (the doc's "no beach access" already matches Creslin's sheer-drop treatment; no new logic needed).
- **South (treeline bordering Creslin's Field)** — a gentle down-slope to **flush terrain height at the
  shared portal pair** (Thornwood's south edge must meet Creslin's north edge at the same world Y so the
  transition doesn't have a visible step) + a treeline dressing (denser than Creslin's north treeline, per
  "canopy blocks light" — reuse the two-row treeline placer, tighter spacing).
- **North (deep forest, zone edge)** — a new, simpler profile: gentle rise, **no portal treatment** (unlike
  Creslin's north, which exists to frame *its* portal) — just a hard forest wall (dense trees, no gap) since
  the doc gives this edge no exit.
- **Interior** — same rolling-hills fbm as Creslin's, but denser tree cover throughout (dense forest, not open
  field) via `Tools/Zones/Add Synty Trees to Terrain` + Unity's Paint Trees (visual density; boundary trees
  still placed with colliders for the actual walls, per the existing "painted trees don't reliably carve
  navmesh" caveat).

### TW4 — Grukmar's Deep entrance placement — **RECOMMEND: move it into the east mountain notch**
Today `Portal_To_Grukmar` sits due north of Thornwood's default entry — convenient scaffolding, not lore. The
doc places it "carved into the mountain face, eastern boundary, mid-north position." Move the portal (and
retarget the `from_grukmar` return entry to sit just west of it, facing away from the rock face) into the
notch cut in TW3. Cheap (transform repositioning, same pattern `CreslinsFieldSetup.MovePortalNorth` already
uses) and meaningfully improves the "prove the framework with real content" bar — an entrance you have to
walk into dense forest to find, not one sitting on the spawn line. *(The doc also wants the entrance "not
immediately visible from the zone transition" — satisfied for free by the notch being mid-north while arrival
is south.)*

### TW5 — MVP mob roster: goblins only — **RECOMMEND**
Two mobs cover the doc's actual content-authoring priority #4 ("Thornwood goblin patrol routes and spawn
variance") without new art work:
- **Reuse `Goblin Scout`** (existing DB mob, body `Goblin_Male_01`, currently level 2) for the southern-edge
  band — bump its level to sit in the doc's 6-9 range (a shared mob, so this also raises its Creslin's Field
  difficulty; acceptable since Creslin's Field's own difficulty gradient already anticipates "goblin scouts
  crossing over from Thornwood" in its eastern hills — see the doc's Zone 1 population notes).
- **New mob `Goblin Warrior`** — body `Goblin_Warrior_Male_01` (one body for MVP; `Goblin_Warrior_Female_01`
  is a trivial second catalog entry later if wanted), level ~7-8, for the organized patrol nearer the Grukmar
  notch.
- Shaman/Warchief bodies exist in the same pack but are explicitly Zone 3 population — not spawned here.

### TW6 — Patrol + spawn design — **RECOMMEND**
Two `PatrolRoute`s (via `Tools/Zones/New Patrol Route` + `Add Patrol Waypoint`, 3-5 waypoints each, one
looping the southern treeline band, one looping nearer the Grukmar notch) each fed by a `SpawnPoint` (via
`Place Encounter`) whose `spawnTableId` points at a small weighted table with `groupSize: 2-3` (approximates
"moving in groups of 2-4" — see the current-state note on why this is independent-members-on-a-shared-route,
not tight formation; acceptable approximation, no engine change needed). A third `SpawnPoint` with a bounded
`WanderRegion` (not a patrol) gives a couple of solitary scouts roaming the southern band between the two
routes, so the forest doesn't read as *only* patrol lines.

### TW7 — Difficulty gradient: southern band only for MVP — **RECOMMEND**
Implement just the doc's "southern treeline edge: levels 6-9" band (TW5's two mob levels). Mid-forest (8-12),
northern deep forest (10-14), and entrance-approach (12-15) bands are meaningless to build now — there's no
leveling content or combat-rework (5.1) yet to make a 4-tier gradient legible or survivable, and Grukmar's
Deep (where the gradient's top end continues) isn't built. Revisit when 3.6 or a later content pass extends
Thornwood's population.

### TW8 — Zone sweeper / wildlife: deferred, no owned art — **DECIDED (blocked on assets)**
No wolf/bear/predator body exists in any imported Synty pack (checked `PolygonDungeon`, `PolygonFantasyRivals`,
`PolygonAdventure`, `PolygonGeneric` — goblins/undead/knights/golems/ghosts only). The doc itself leaves the
"Thornwood zone sweeper species" as an **open question**, so this isn't blocking a decided design — it's
blocking an owned asset. Skip entirely for 3.5; log to backlog (needs a nature/creature Synty pack, same
posture as the earlier Elf-race deferral in 3.1.4).

### TW9 — Zone-boundary state: nothing new needed — **CONFIRMED, not a build item**
The roadmap's original 3.5 framing ("defines what state crosses zone boundaries in practice") predates
3.0/3.0.2's actual build. It's already answered: `zone_id` persists per character (0017), spawn/corpse/
death-respawn/`/unstuck` are all zone-aware (3.0 Stage C), chat delivers zone-agnostically via `conn.Send`
(3.0 Stage A), and a mob disengages when its target zones out (3.1.11 WR7). 3.5 doesn't add engineering here
— the test plan just re-confirms these hold with **real, simultaneous content in two zones** (a goblin patrol
+ the Creslin's Field encounters), not the single placeholder rat that's exercised them so far.

## Build / test plan
1. **Shape Thornwood**: write `ThornwoodTerrainSetup.cs` (+ extract `TerrainShapingUtil.cs` per TW2) → new
   menu `Tools/Zones/Build Thornwood Terrain`. Open `thornwood.unity` directly (never re-run `Build Zone
   Scenes`). Run it; verify the four boundaries read correctly in Scene view (mountain wall + notch east,
   cliff/sea west, flush-height treeline transition south, dense hard-walled forest north) and the south edge
   meets Creslin's Field's north edge at the same height across the shared portal pair.
2. **Reposition the Grukmar portal** (TW4) into the east notch; verify `from_grukmar`'s arrival facing reads
   sensibly walking back out.
3. **Dress**: `Add Synty Trees to Terrain` + Paint Trees for canopy density; keep hand-placed boundary trees
   (with colliders) at the true walls. Rebake + persist the NavMesh (mountain/cliff self-exclude via slope).
4. **Author content** (web editors + in-scene tooling): bump `Goblin Scout`'s level; create DB mob `Goblin
   Warrior`; wire both bodies in `MobModelCatalog` (`Goblin Scout`→`Goblin_Male_01` likely already mapped from
   3.1.10 — confirm; add `Goblin Warrior`→`Goblin_Warrior_Male_01`); author a weighted spawn table with
   `groupSize`; place 2 `PatrolRoute`s + 3 `SpawnPoint`s (2 patrol-fed, 1 wander-bounded) via `EncounterTools`.
5. **Save** the scene (gains the terrain + content).
6. **Playtest** (Host + remote MPPM client per the established pattern): walk creslins→thornwood, confirm the
   forest/mountain/cliff read + no seam at the portal; find and engage a goblin patrol (group spawn, shared
   route, correct level); walk to the Grukmar notch and confirm the portal round-trips; re-verify per-zone
   isolation, corpse-in-zone, death respawn, and login-into-persisted-zone specifically with the new content
   present (TW9) — not just the rat.

## Done when
Thornwood reads as an actual forest zone (shaped terrain matching all four boundaries, dense canopy, a
functioning goblin-patrol encounter with real spawn variance) instead of a flat scaffold — proving the
3.1.9–3.1.11 tooling generalizes to a second zone — and the creslins⇄thornwood round-trip + zone isolation
still hold with real, simultaneous content in both zones. Deeper bands, wildlife, named encounters, and
Grukmar's Deep's own population are 3.6 / future content passes.

## Implementation log (2026-07-12)
Implemented as TW1–TW9 recommended, code only — not compiled/tested in-editor (no Unity instance available to
this session; every step below is "pending in-editor verification" per this project's usual pattern).

- **NEW `Assets/Scripts/Editor/TerrainShapingUtil.cs`** (TW2) — the shared, proven-stable primitives (`Fbm`,
  `SStep`, `LoadAll`, `StripColliders`, `MeasureFootprint`, `FindType`, `SetEnum`, `SurfaceY`) plus a new
  `BakeAndPersistTerrainNavMesh(host, navAssetPath)` that adds/bakes a whole-scene `NavMeshSurface` and
  persists the `NavMeshData` to an asset — needed because Thornwood is additively loaded (unlike Creslin's
  Field's always-loaded base scene), so an unpersisted bake would leave the zone with no navmesh on reload
  (the exact 3.0.1 bug). **`TerrainZoneSetup.cs` (Creslin's Field) was deliberately left untouched** rather
  than retrofitted to share this file — a pragmatic call favoring zero regression risk on an already-verified,
  hard-won pipeline over eliminating a small amount of duplication (a mild deviation from TW2's original
  phrasing, noted here rather than silently).
- **EDIT `Assets/Scripts/Editor/TerrainTextureSetup.cs`** — `ApplyToTerrain`/`LoadLayers`/`LoadLocalLayer`/
  `DefaultTerrainLitMaterial` gained an `assetDir` parameter (default = the existing Creslin's Field path, so
  the existing `Apply()` menu item and `TerrainZoneSetup.Build()` call site are unaffected). Without this,
  Thornwood's terrain would have shared — and fought over — Creslin's Field's terrain-layer/material assets.
- **NEW `Assets/Scripts/Editor/ThornwoodTerrainSetup.cs`** (TW2/TW3/TW4) — `Tools/Zones/Build Thornwood
  Terrain` (+ `Clear`). 700×820u procedural terrain anchored off the zone's own `ZoneEntry("default")`
  (Thornwood has no `NetworkStartPosition`): east mountain wall with a carved walkable saddle mid-north
  (the Grukmar's Deep notch), west sheer cliff to the sea (no beach), dense south treeline with a gap only at
  the Creslin's-portal corridor, hard dense north wall (no gap — no exit there). Removes the 3.0.2 flat
  `thornwood_Ground` scaffold, re-snaps existing entry/portal markers onto the new surface, and **moves the
  Grukmar's Deep portal + its return entry into the east-wall notch** (TW4). Bakes + persists the NavMesh
  (`thornwood_navmesh.asset`, same path 3.0.2 used) after border props are placed.
- **NEW `Assets/Scripts/Editor/ThornwoodEncounterSetup.cs`** (TW5/TW6) — `Tools/Zones/Build Thornwood
  Encounters`. Wires `Goblin Warrior` (new) into `MobModelCatalog` (`SM_Chr_Goblin_Warrior_Male_01`, plus a
  defensive re-affirm of `Goblin Scout`'s existing mapping), removes the placeholder `RatSpawn`, and places a
  southern patrol route (group-3 `Thornwood Goblin Patrol` table) + an approach patrol route nearer the
  Grukmar notch (group-2 `Thornwood Warrior Patrol` table) + a solitary wander-bounded `Goblin Scout` between
  them. All waypoints/spawn/wander-region positions snap onto the real terrain surface (NavMesh sample with a
  raycast fallback — the rolling terrain varies far more than the flat scaffold it replaces).
- **EDIT `Assets/Scripts/Server/Database/DatabaseSeeder.cs`** — `SeedSpawnEntry` gained an optional
  `groupSize` parameter (backward-compatible; existing calls default to 1). New `SeedThornwoodEncounters`
  (called from `Seed()`, after `SeedExampleEncounters` so the `Monster` faction + `Goblin Scout` already
  exist): seeds the `Goblin Warrior` mob (level 7, Monster faction) and the two new spawn tables above. Per
  this codebase's established seeding rule (**"ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING so edits made in the web editors are
  never overwritten"**), bumping the *existing* `Goblin Scout`'s level to the doc's 6-9 southern-band range is
  **not** done via seed — it's a one-field manual edit in the web Mob Editor (logged in the tool's own NEXT
  message), consistent with how this project treats seeds as one-time bootstraps, not sync targets.
