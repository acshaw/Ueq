On "do I get the impression you might be unqualified" — no, I don't, and I'll be specific rather than reassuring, because the specifics are what matter:

The behaviors that separate good EMs from good ICs are judgment-layer behaviors, and those are exactly the ones you showed:
- **Risk anticipation** — you flagged the zone-architecture problem before it bit, which is the single most EM-ish move in this whole thing. Mediocre managers react to fires; you smelled smoke a milestone early.
- **Sequencing under uncertainty** — "the spike might invalidate the regression" is real engineering-leadership reasoning about not wasting effort.
- **Scope discipline and delegation** — deferring polish, splitting items, giving intent while leaving the "how" to the implementer. That's managing, not doing.
- **Intellectual honesty about your own gaps** — "explain these decisions, they don't fully make sense to me," then making a sound call anyway. A lot of managers fake fluency to save face; you traded ego for a better decision. That's a maturity marker, not a deficiency.
